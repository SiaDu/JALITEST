# User-study analysis summary

## Formal dataset
- **Included:** U01–U10 (N=10 usable questionnaire records).
- **Excluded as incomplete:** U0, U11.
- **Excluded from formal analysis:** U12–U16 synthetic supervisor-preview records.
- Seven participants reported a numeric experience duration: 2–8 years, M=4.57, SD=1.99.

## Study A — Authoring workflow
- Match to intended reading: Direct **5.10 ± 0.97**, Tags **5.65 ± 0.82**; W=9, p=.117, Holm p=.219, r_rb=.60.
- Final satisfaction: Direct **5.15 ± 1.06**, Tags **5.75 ± 0.89**; W=4, p=.109, Holm p=.219, r_rb=.71.
- Largest workflow-item shift: revision clarity, **4.89 → 6.22**; unadjusted p=.063, Holm p=.563, r_rb=1.00.
- No workflow item or NASA-TLX dimension remained significant after Holm correction.
- Diagnostics: tag edit-level match **5.00 ± 1.05**; phrase interpretation helpfulness **5.30 ± 1.34**; dual-character layout **6.20 ± 0.63**.
- **Design limitation:** collected authoring sessions used Direct Generation before editable tags, so order/practice is confounded with workflow.

## Study B — Context ablation
- Overall scene fit: Dialogue-Only **5.22 ± 0.90**, Full-Context **5.58 ± 1.02**; W=0, p=.0078, Holm p=.039, r_rb=1.00.
- Intention, affect, gaze, and timing did not remain significant after Holm correction.
- Preferences across 60 scene trials: Full-Context **27**, Dialogue-Only **15**, No preference **11**, missing **6**, ambiguous **1**.
- Among the 42 determinate A/B choices, Full-Context = **64.3%**.
