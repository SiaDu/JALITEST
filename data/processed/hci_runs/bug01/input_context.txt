Marty, Rachel, and Dion are in a bedroom at Dion's family apartment. Dion shows Marty the orange table-tennis balls and packaging he has produced for their Marty Supreme business.

Story:
Marty has recently returned after being away for months, leaving Dion with a large amount of unsold inventory. Dion and his father have nevertheless continued investing money and effort into Marty's business. Rachel has come with Marty because she is dealing with a difficult situation at home and needs somewhere to stay.

Character:
MARTY is intensely ambitious and emotionally energized by anything that validates his table-tennis career and business ambitions.
DION is Marty's loyal business partner. He wants Marty to recognize the work and investment he has put into their project.
RACHEL is in a vulnerable and unsettled personal situation. Her immediate concerns are very different from Marty's excitement about the business.

Performance Context:
Marty becomes genuinely excited and impressed by Dion's work and seeks Rachel's agreement with his enthusiasm. Dion is pleased to finally receive Marty's recognition. Rachel participates politely, but she does not share their level of excitement because her attention and emotional priorities are elsewhere. Her positive response therefore carries less enthusiasm than its literal wording suggests.
