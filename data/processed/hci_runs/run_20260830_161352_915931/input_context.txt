Will and Agnes meet for the first time in the garden at Hewlands. Will has just left the study where he is tutoring the boys after noticing Agnes outside with her hawk.

Story:
Will is working as a Latin tutor at Hewlands to help repay his father's debt. He feels trapped by his circumstances. Agnes lives at Hewlands and has an independent, unconventional relationship with the natural world.

Character:
WILL is educated, restless, curious, and somewhat socially uncertain around Agnes.
AGNES is independent, intuitive, self-possessed, and cautious about this unfamiliar man.

Performance Context:
There is an immediate mutual attraction, but neither character knows the other yet. Their conversation is tentative and slightly awkward. Will is searching for a reason to remain near Agnes, while Agnes is curious about why he approached her. The hawk provides a safer subject through which they can continue the interaction.
